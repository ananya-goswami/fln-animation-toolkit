Sound effects used by the FLN Animation Kit.

  correct.ogg   Kenney "Jingles" pack, jingles_SAX03.ogg  -  CC0 1.0 (public domain,
                no attribution required).  kenney.nl
                Native pitch - nothing is transposed. Trimmed to where its useful
                energy ends (1.035s), peak-normalised to 0.89, 160ms fade.
                Rises 10.9 semitones - verified by pitch contour, not by filename.
                Picked by measuring the preview reel noted below and matching it on
                length (1.05s -> 1.00s) and attack (99ms -> 100ms).

  wrong.ogg     "universfield-wrong-answer-129254" (supplied by the team, Pixabay).
                Original is 3.29s but its useful energy ends at 0.95s, so it is
                trimmed to 1.05s - untrimmed it ran into the next question.
                Peak-normalised to 0.89, 170ms fade. Falls 4.8 semitones.

  burst.ogg     "freesound_community-pick-92276" (supplied by the team, Freesound).
                Original carries 512ms of LEADING SILENCE, which made every star pop
                arrive half a second after the tap; trimmed to the onset, 0.40s.
                Used for the landing-screen stars popping.

Check the licence terms of the supplied downloads before shipping - they arrived
ready-made and their exact terms are not recorded here. The Kenney file is CC0 and
needs nothing.

NOT USED, and why:
  "preview.mp3"  -  a 16.8s stock-marketplace preview reel of eight takes. This is
  the sound the team liked, and correct.ogg above was chosen specifically to match
  it, but the reel itself cannot ship: it carries a spoken watermark, and that
  watermark is the marker saying the file has not been licensed. Stripping the
  voiceover would not make it licensed. If the track is bought, drop the clean
  download in as correct.ogg and delete this paragraph.
