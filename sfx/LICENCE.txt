Sound effects used by the FLN Animation Kit.

  correct.ogg   Kenney "Interface Sounds", question_003.ogg  -  CC0 1.0 (public
                domain, no attribution required).  kenney.nl
                Transposed down to 0.65x so it sits in a warm register instead of
                its native 4440Hz brightness, peak-normalised to 0.89, 130ms fade.
                Rises 10.7 semitones - verified by pitch contour, not by filename.

  wrong.ogg     "universfield-wrong-answer-129254" (supplied by the team, Pixabay).
                Original is 3.29s but its useful energy ends at 0.95s, so it is
                trimmed to 1.05s - untrimmed it ran into the next question.
                Peak-normalised to 0.89, 170ms fade. Falls 5.1 semitones.

  burst.ogg     "freesound_community-pick-92276" (supplied by the team, Freesound).
                Original carries 512ms of LEADING SILENCE, which made every star pop
                arrive half a second after the tap; trimmed to the onset, 0.40s.
                Used for the landing-screen stars popping.

Check the licence terms of the supplied downloads before shipping - they arrived
ready-made and their exact terms are not recorded here. The Kenney file is CC0 and
needs nothing.

NOT USED, and why:
  A stock preview reel ("preview.mp3") was briefly the correct-answer sound. It
  carries a spoken watermark. A watermarked preview is by definition not the
  licensed asset, so it cannot ship whatever it sounds like - correct.ogg is the
  CC0 file above instead.

  celebrate     the fleet's own assets/Audio/sfx_celebrate.ogg (MTKGA02's version,
