Sound effects used by the FLN Animation Kit (recipe 22).

  correct.ogg   take 3 of "preview.mp3" (supplied by the team; Pixabay-style
                preview reel of 8 takes). Take 3 is the one that RISES (+7
                semitones) - most of the takes in that reel fall.
                Trimmed to 1.15s, peak-normalised to 0.89, 170ms fade.

  wrong.ogg     "universfield-wrong-answer-129254" (supplied by the team, Pixabay).
                Original is 3.29s but its useful energy ends at 0.95s, so it is
                trimmed to 1.05s - untrimmed it ran into the next question.
                Peak-normalised to 0.89, 170ms fade.

  burst.ogg     "freesound_community-pick-92276" (supplied by the team, Freesound).
                Original carries 512ms of LEADING SILENCE, which would have made
                every star pop arrive half a second after the tap; trimmed to the
                onset, 0.40s, peak-normalised to 0.89.
                Used for every "elements burst apart" moment: the landing-screen
                stars, the end-screen burst, and the correct-answer confetti.

Check the licence terms of the original downloads before shipping - they were
supplied ready-made and their exact terms are not recorded here.

Not in this folder:
  celebrate     the fleet's own assets/Audio/sfx_celebrate.ogg (MTKGA02's version,
                the fullest of the six in the fleet). Registered but not currently
                fired - the end screen uses burst.ogg instead.

Earlier CC0 files from Kenney (kenney.nl) were removed: they came from an
*interface* pack, so they were synthesised UI blips and read as machine-made.
